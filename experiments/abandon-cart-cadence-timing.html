PAT_AbandonCartUnverified | Cadence & Timing

Owner: Leah Ran: 2026-05-13 → 2026-06-10 Primary metric: Cohort engagement + CVR (multiple)
POSITIVE

Hypothesis

Capitalizing on recency with a shorter, more aggressive outreach cadence (10 days vs. the current 21 days) will improve CVR and engagement metrics in the abandon-cart series.
Target impact: Produce a statistically significant increase to cohort engagement metrics (OR, CTR, CTO) and CVR metrics (eligibility checks, bookings, funnel returns).

Experiment Details

The unverified abandon-cart campaign runs on an extended 21-day cadence that may leave conversion opportunity on the table. This test evaluates whether a shorter, more aggressive 10-day cadence improves CVR and engagement. Only timing/cadence changed — creative, channel mix, and touchpoint sequence stayed the same. Analyzed at 28 days to give the control cohort's full CVR window time to mature.

Impact summary

A compressed 10-day cadence outperformed the existing 21-day sequence across most key metrics. Engagement (clickers, click-to-open, funnel re-entry) hit statistical significance; downstream conversion (eligibility checks, bookings) trended positive but didn't reach significance. Cadence and timing are now considered optimized — future testing shifts to subject line, messaging, and content.

Learnings

What worked

  • Shortened cadence drove stat-sig lifts in openers, clickers, multi-clickers, click-to-open rate, and funnel re-entry — recency and urgency meaningfully increased engagement and action.
  • CTR and CTO improved at every touchpoint despite open rates staying flat, meaning condensed timing converts already-engaged users better without needing more opens.

What didn't

  • Downstream CVR (eligibility checks, bookings) trended positive but didn't reach significance, possibly due to attribution lag.
  • Open rates were unchanged — cadence alone doesn't grow top-of-funnel awareness.

Follow-up actions

  1. Roll the 10-day cadence into the abandoned-cart series as the new standard.
  2. Shift testing focus to subject line / preview header optimization — open rate is the next lever.
  3. Test copy, CTA, and creative variation now that timing is set.
