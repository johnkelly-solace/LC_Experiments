PAT_AbandonCartVerified | Super Test

Owner team: Early Lifecycle Ran: 2026-06-17 → 2026-07-21 Primary metric: CVR (CHW_INTRO_CALL_BOOKED), CVR (PATIENT_ATTENDED_CALL), OR, CTR, CTO
POSITIVE

Hypothesis

H1: Cohort B (new series) will achieve a higher conversion rate than Cohort A (legacy series). H2: Cohort B (new series) will achieve higher campaign engagement metrics than Cohort A (legacy series).
Target impact: 15%+ increase to CVR and to engagement metrics (OR, CTR, CTO).

Experiment Details

Abandon-cart campaigns had gone stale — dated, text-based, repetitive messaging. Following the cadence tests on the Unverified flow and the rollout of the content flywheel, this is the “Verified” sibling of that same overhaul: a fully redesigned series goes head-to-head against the legacy series in a Super Test to establish a new baseline, before returning to incremental A/B optimization. What changed all at once: text-based templates → designed templates; 15-min trigger → 8-min trigger (closer to the 5.3-min median time-to-convert); “Jamie” sender → “Solace Health” sender; 5 emails + 3 SMS → 4 emails + 2 SMS; 21-day series → 10-day series; simultaneous email/SMS sends → staggered sends; soft messaging → CVR-focused messaging; SMS send-window loosened from weekdays-only to every day, 9:45am–6pm.

Impact summary

Super Test B (New) beat A (Old) decisively on both hypotheses, clearing the 15% target on every primary and engagement metric while running a shorter, leaner series: CHW_INTRO_CALL_BOOKED CVR +34.6%, PATIENT_ATTENDED_CALL CVR +39.5%, OR +7.3%, CTR +29.6%, CTO +20.8% (all stat sig). Unsub rate also rose (+23.9%, stat sig) — small in absolute terms but worth tracking.

Learnings

What worked

  • The new series won broadly even accounting for the faster T1 trigger — excluding T1 entirely, B still beat A by +15% aggregate CVR across the remaining touchpoints, confirming the redesign drives real lift, not just faster timing.
  • It's not just speed — B's T1 click-to-open rate was +13% higher than A's, meaning the new creative and CTA engaged people more effectively once they opened it.
  • The redesigned EM2 (new subject line/timing) was a standout, with open rate +7.2% over control.
  • SMS2 converted better (+2% CVR) with cleaner deliverability than control.
  • A follow-up analysis (booked-to-human-open instead of booked-to-sent) confirmed the lift holds even isolating for engaged users only and excluding T1's timing advantage entirely (+15.1% higher booking rate on EM2–EM4 opens alone) — a genuine content/design win, not just a timing artifact.
  • This mirrors the Unverified flow's own Super Test (same redesign playbook, run on the sibling campaign) — both landed decisively positive, so this playbook (modern templates, faster trigger, staggered sends, direct CVR-focused copy) generalizes across the verified/unverified abandon-cart split, not just one campaign.

What didn't

  • EM3's open rate dropped sharply (-7.56% vs. control) — since its click-to-open was actually better, this looks like a subject-line or cadence-gap issue rather than a content problem.
  • SMS1 underperformed control on both CTR (-4.02%) and CVR (-2.12%) — the weakest touchpoint in the new series.
  • Unsubscribe rate rose +23.9% (stat sig) — still small in absolute terms (0.20% vs. 0.16%), but worth monitoring at scale.

Follow-up actions

  1. Promote the Super Test path to the default, retire the legacy series, and shift to targeted A/B tests on EM3 and SMS1 specifically.
  2. Test subject lines for EM3 and evaluate whether the EM2→EM3 cadence gap needs adjusting.
  3. Rework SMS1 copy/CTA via an A/B test against the current control.
  4. Add subject-line and content (video/education) A/B tests to keep optimizing engagement.

Additional notes

  • Leah Sternberger — July 21, 2026Re-checked whether B's win was just the faster T1 trigger by measuring booked-to-human-open ratio instead of booked-to-sent. B still converted engaged opens into bookings at a significantly higher rate than A, both across the full series (11.88% vs. 9.32%, +27.5%) and even with T1 excluded entirely (7.49% vs. 6.51%, +15.1%) — confirming the content/design changes drive real lift independent of the timing head start.
