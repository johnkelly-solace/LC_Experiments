PAT_AbandonCartUnverified | Warm Leads vs. Education End Points

Owner: Leah Ran: 2026-07-14 → 2026-08-05 Primary metric: CTR: Human Clicks (primary); CVR: Eligibility Check (secondary)
POSITIVE

Hypothesis

Prospects from PAT_AbandonCartUnverified who drain into the new EDU flywheel campaign (PAT_EducationContentFlywheel) will re-engage at higher rates (human clicks) than prospects who enter the old drip campaign (PAT_WarmLeads).
Target impact: +15% increase to reactivations (human clicks).

Experiment Details

Splits traffic from PAT_AbandonCartUnverified 50/50 between the legacy Warm Leads drip and the new Education Content Flywheel campaign, to see which re-engages unverified prospects more effectively. Third experiment in this campaign family, following the Cadence & Timing test and the full-series Super Test.

Impact summary

Moving unverified prospects into the education flywheel campaign more than tripled human click engagement (+245.6%, stat sig) vs. legacy warm leads. Downstream CVR events trended positive across the board but hadn't reached significance when the test closed. Rerouting all abandon-cart prospects into the education flywheel going forward.

Learnings

What worked

  • Routing unverified prospects to the education flywheel instead of legacy warm leads more than tripled human click engagement (+245.6%, stat sig).
  • Every downstream CVR event measured trended positive in the same direction — funnel re-entry +11.2%, eligibility check +1.1%, CHW intro call booked +14.6%, patient attended call +17.7% — even though none individually reached significance yet.
  • Third win in a row for this same campaign family (Cadence & Timing → Super Test → this test) — reinforces that PAT_AbandonCartUnverified responds well to sustained iteration.

What didn't

  • None of the downstream CVR metrics reached statistical significance before the test closed.

Follow-up actions

  1. Reroute all abandon-cart prospects into the education flywheel campaign instead of warm leads, given the consistent directional lift plus the stat-sig engagement win.
  2. Worth checking whether this interacts with the redesigned Super Test series already rolled out on this same campaign.

Additional notes

  • August 5, 2026Closed early rather than waiting for CVR to reach significance — legacy Warm Leads prospects are being rerouted into the education flywheel regardless, which would have nulled the test's control group anyway.
