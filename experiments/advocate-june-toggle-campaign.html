Advocate June Toggle Campaign

Owner team: Advocate Lifecycle Ran: 2026-06-18 → 2026-06-25 Primary metric: ADVOCATE_TOGGLED_AVAILABILITY (listing_status = unpaused)
POSITIVE

Hypothesis

Advocates who receive email comms will be more likely to toggle their availability back on than those who don't. Highly-engaged advocates will be more likely to take action than medium- or low-engaged advocates.

Experiment Details

Targets a list of advocates to test whether email communications have a positive effect on getting advocates to toggle their availability back on (“unpause”), and whether engagement level moderates the effect.

Impact summary

The single biggest win in this dataset: a simple reactivation email drove advocates to unpause at 3–5.7x the rate of holdout, highly statistically significant in both reads. Note: this result lives in the experiment page's write-up but hadn't been copied into the database's Result/Impact Summary fields — worth fixing in Notion so this doesn't get missed.

Learnings

What worked

  • A simple reactivation email drove a massive, highly significant increase in advocates unpausing their availability across two separate reads.

What didn't

  • Couldn't compare high vs. medium vs. low engagement segments — the upload list had bounces/suppressions that broke the segmentation.

Follow-up actions

  1. Run a proper engagement-tier test (high/medium/low) to see if the effect concentrates in any one group.
  2. Update the Notion database Result/Impact Summary fields for this experiment — currently blank despite a strong, well-documented result on the page itself.

Additional notes

  • Couldn't compare high/medium/low engagement tiers this round — the upload list had bounces/suppressions that broke segmentation. Good candidate for a follow-up test.
