Appointment · PAT_CallConfirmationFlowSMS

Confirmation SMS Reschedule

Owner: Chris Ran: 2026-02-26 → 2026-05-12 Primary metric: Intro call attendance rate (show rate)
POSITIVE

Hypothesis

By providing a reschedule option in the confirmation message, we might avoid pure cancellations without a reschedule.

Experiment Details

Adds a reschedule option/link to the appointment confirmation flow (follow-up call confirmation + SMS1) to reduce pure cancellations.

Impact summary

Adding a reschedule option lifted attendance +4-4.5% across both the follow-up call confirmation and the SMS1 confirmation touchpoints — likely by capturing patients who would otherwise have just cancelled outright.

Learnings

What worked

  • Adding a reschedule option lifted attendance across both touchpoints tested, with high confidence.

What didn't

  • No notable misses.

Follow-up actions

  1. Closed out both tests and banked the change; moved on to the next appointment-flow test.

Additional notes

  • 95% confidence on the follow-up variant, 99% confidence on the SMS1 variant. Closing both out.
