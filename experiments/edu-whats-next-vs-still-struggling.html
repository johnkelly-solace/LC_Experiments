EDU “What's Next” PHYS Intake Content vs. “Still Struggling”

Owner: Leah Ran: 2026-04-22 → 2026-05-29 Primary metric: OR, CTR, CVR (physician intro call booked)
POSITIVE

Hypothesis

If we replace the underperforming mid-series “still struggling?” email with educational content addressing what patients can expect from the physician intake call, we'll reduce hesitancy and improve engagement and CVR (bookings).

Impact summary

Educational “what's next” content significantly outperformed control across every metric — all statistically significant. Meeting patients with clear immediate next steps beat selling long-term benefits or naming the overwhelm directly.

Learnings

What worked

  • Educational “what's next” content resonated far better than overwhelm-focused messaging.
  • A name-first subject line (“[Name], you're approved — here's what's next”) likely contributed to the open-rate lift.
  • Meeting patients where they are (immediate next steps) proved more relevant than selling end benefits.

What didn't

  • No notable misses.

Follow-up actions

  1. Extend the “what's next” framing to other mid-series touchpoints across lifecycle stages.
