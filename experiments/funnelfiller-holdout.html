Impact summary

The holdout test showed the Funnel Filler campaign drives CVR lift. Across all segments, there was a stat-sig increase of +81.9% in eligibility checks and +128.7% in booked calls versus the control group. Gains held across segments, with unverified (+123% e-checks & +115% bookings) and verified (+137% bookings). The campaign had good engagement (18–24% email opens, >40% SMS CTR). Based on these results, the campaign will be rolled out to the remaining 206,721 holdout users on 8/17.

Learnings

What worked

  • Sending the campaign drove stat-sig CVR lift across every measured segment — Total (+81.9% eligibility checks, +128.7% bookings), Unverified (+123.5% e-checks, +115.2% bookings), Verified (+137.5% bookings).
  • Waitlist segment saw a huge directional jump in funnel-return CVR (+1,139.5%, stat sig even on a small base), suggesting this campaign is especially good at pulling lapsed waitlisted prospects back in.
  • Engagement was solid across the board — 18-24% email opens, 40%+ SMS CTR — supporting the CVR story rather than contradicting it.

What didn't

  • Waitlist's e-check (+46.9%) and booking (+32.2%) lifts were directionally positive but not stat sig — can't be used with certainty despite the strong funnel-return number.

Follow-up actions

  1. Deploy the campaign to the remaining 206,721 holdout users on 8/17 — already actioned same-day given how decisive the result was.
