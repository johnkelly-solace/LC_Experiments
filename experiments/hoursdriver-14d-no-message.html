Impact summary

Ended slightly positive after the 14-day maturation window. Stronger significance showed up on engagement (attended call) than on the primary CVR metric — a possible engagement driver worth revisiting.

Learnings

What worked

  • Engagement (attended call) showed a clearer lift than the primary conversion metric.

What didn't

  • Primary CVR metric (hours submitted) landed close to flat.

Follow-up actions

  1. Revisit as a potential engagement driver rather than a pure conversion play.
