Impact summary

Outreach increased conversion versus holdout (final read: 90.7% vs. 85.5%), suggesting messaging drives incremental conversions for this segment. Small sample, but directionally clear.

Learnings

What worked

  • Active patients with a portal account responded to the reminder with a meaningfully higher conversion rate.

What didn't

  • Sample size is small — treat as directional rather than conclusive.

Follow-up actions

  1. Combine learnings with the month-start holdout test to standardize a recurring active-patient nudge.
