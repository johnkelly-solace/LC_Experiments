Impact summary

Ran for 6 days; treatment performed slightly worse than holdout, essentially flat. For relatively active users without a portal account, this outreach had no measurable impact — these users likely convert regardless of messaging.

Learnings

What worked

  • Nothing notable worked here.

What didn't

  • No measurable lift for this segment — nearly identical conversion with or without the message.

Follow-up actions

  1. Don't treat this as proof inactive patients can't be moved — see the later Inactive Hours Driver A/B/Holdout test, where emotional copy did move this audience.
