Hypothesis

If patients receive a beginning-of-month reminder, they'll complete more patient hours during the month than those who don't receive the reminder.
Target impact: Increase patient hours.

Experiment Details

Two holdout tests measuring the incremental impact of sending a beginning-of-month reminder to patients at risk of falling short on their monthly hours, encouraging them to meet with their advocate and stay on track.

Impact summary

Patients who received the beginning-of-month reminder converted at a higher rate than the holdout group (62.6% vs. 58.3%), a lift of +7.4%. Directionally positive, not stat sig at 95% — worth a larger/longer follow-up.

Learnings

What worked

  • Beginning-of-month reminder drove more conversions than holdout, suggesting it helps motivate at-risk patients to engage with their advocate.

What didn't

  • The lift wasn't statistically significant, so the improvement can't be confidently separated from normal variation yet.

Follow-up actions

  1. Continue testing with a larger sample size and/or longer duration to increase statistical power.
  2. Test alternative copy, timing, or audience segments to find where reminders have the most impact.
