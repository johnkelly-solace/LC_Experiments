MPAT_PhysIntroCallNoShowWnb | Improved Winback Flow

Owner: Chris Ran: 2026-05-29 → 2026-06-15 Primary metric: Patient attendance for physician call
POSITIVE

Hypothesis

The current flow is too long and doesn't engage patients well enough in the initial touchpoints. By introducing more engaging touchpoints with better channel orchestration and cadence, we'll improve winback of no-show patients.
Target impact: +50% improved attendance rate for no-show patients.

Experiment Details

Restructuring the follow-up cadence and frequency to improve short-term connection rates for no-show patients.

Impact summary

+27% relative lift in attendance for the optimized flow. Hit stat significance quickly and held over 2+ weeks with decent volume — the strongest, most durable win in the Appointment category.

Learnings

What worked

  • Combining multiple flow elements (more engaging touchpoints + better channel orchestration/cadence) jumped the baseline enough to unlock more nuanced follow-up testing.
  • Prioritizing SMS over simultaneous email+SMS boosted SMS conversion, at some cost to email conversion — net positive.

What didn't

  • No notable misses.

Follow-up actions

  1. Adopt these winback strategies across other flows (cancellation / unmatched / etc.)
  2. Run more nuanced testing on top of this new, higher baseline.
