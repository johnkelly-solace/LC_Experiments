PAT Inactive Hours Driver | A/B/Holdout

Owner: Brooke Ran: 2026-06-18 → 2026-06-18 Primary metric: TIME_SPAN_SUBMITTED
POSITIVE

Hypothesis

If inactive patients receive gentle reminders, it will encourage them to schedule a check-in with their advocate and re-engage.
Target impact: Increase patient hours / meetings with advocates.

Experiment Details

After seeing early wins with beginning-of-month hours drivers for active patients, this tests a similar concept for inactive patients — a 3-way test assessing copy tone. Path A is more direct and succinct, Path B is more emotionally-driven and sensitive, and C is a holdout.

Impact summary

Emotionally-toned copy drove 44% more conversions than the plain/normal-tone variant (33% higher than holdout), at 92% confidence. Volume was lower than ideal, so treat as a strong early signal rather than a settled result — but it flips the earlier assumption that inactive patients simply don't respond to messaging.

Learnings

What worked

  • Emotionally-driven, sensitive copy clearly outperformed both the plain “normal” variant and the holdout on conversion.

What didn't

  • Emotional copy drove ~50% less email engagement (opens/clicks) than the normal variant even though it converted better — engagement and conversion didn't move together here.

Follow-up actions

  1. Re-confirm the emotional-copy effect at larger scale before rolling out broadly.
