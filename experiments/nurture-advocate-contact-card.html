MPAT_Nurture Campaign | Advocate Contact Card

Owner team: Patient Engagement Ran: 2026-02-13 → 2026-05-21 Primary metric: Time submitted CVR
POSITIVE

Hypothesis

Adding the Advocate contact card after a patient meets with them for the first time will increase engagement and conversion (time submitted).
Target impact: Increase patient hours by including contact card to easily access.

Experiment Details

An A/B test launched in SMS #1 of the Nurture Campaign to test whether adding an advocate contact card increased engagement with patients who completed their first advocate call.

Impact summary

The experiment ran for ~13 weeks and Variant B (with advocate contact card) outperformed the control, resulting in a 16.2% lift. Control 29.7% vs. Treatment 34.5%.

Learnings

What worked

  • Adding the advocate contact card to the post-intro-call SMS lifted conversion +16.2% over a ~13-week run.

What didn't

  • No notable misses.

Follow-up actions

  1. Test the same contact-card addition in other campaigns.
