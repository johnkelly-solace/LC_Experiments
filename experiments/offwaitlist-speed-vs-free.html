Impact summary

“Free” beat “speed” messaging by +2.11% CTR at >90% confidence (not yet 95%). Bookings trended +84% higher for “free” (non-sig, small sample). Moving forward with “free” for additional waitlist removals; recommend further iteration to confirm at 95%.

Learnings

What worked

  • “Free” produced a directional lift over “speed” at >90% confidence.

What didn't

  • Limited audience (batched send capped both variants at ~9.3K) plus lower-than-expected SMS availability/delivery limited statistical power.
  • Eligibility checks completed stayed flat between variants.

Follow-up actions

  1. Move forward with “free” for additional waitlist removals.
  2. Run iteration tests in other unverified prospect campaigns to confirm at 95% confidence.
  3. Evaluate secondary CVR metrics (funnel re-entry, eligibility check starts) once available in HEX.
