Impact summary

Variant B (personas) produced a statistically significant +29.5% increase in CTR over Variant A (tasks) — suggesting playful copy can outperform straightforward messaging even with a commercial audience typically treated like the more conservative Medicare audience.

Learnings

What worked

  • Playful “personas” framing beat straightforward “tasks” framing for advocate capability education.

What didn't

  • No notable misses.

Follow-up actions

  1. Roll out Variant B (personas) to 100% of this audience.
  2. Develop additional playful copy variants to keep testing with commercial audiences.

Additional notes
