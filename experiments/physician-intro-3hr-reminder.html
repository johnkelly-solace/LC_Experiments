Results — control vs. test

No baseline-vs-test breakdown was logged for this experiment — see impact summary above.

Impact summary

Same pattern as its 4hr sibling test: started with a ~15–20% lift that steadily degraded to roughly flat by the end of the test.

Learnings

What worked

  • Early lift matched the 4hr variant's pattern, suggesting the effect is about proximity/urgency generally, not this specific timing.

What didn't

  • Lift faded to flat — same decay pattern as the 4hr reminder.

Follow-up actions

  1. Retest individual touchpoints after the appointment “super test” wraps.
