Impact summary

This touchpoint started with a ~15–20% lift but slowly degraded over time, landing virtually flat by the end of the test.

Learnings

What worked

  • Strong early lift (~15–20%) in the first two weeks.

What didn't

  • Lift decayed steadily and was essentially gone by week four — early results overstated the durable effect.

Follow-up actions

  1. Retest individual touchpoints after the appointment “super test” wraps.

Additional notes

  • Virtually flat after 4 weeks. Next step: retest individual touchpoints after the appointment “super test” completes.
