Impact summary

Flat immediate show-rate impact, but a 42% increase in reschedule rate — netting out to roughly a +2% boost in overall attendance once rescheduled patients are counted. The test had to be restarted mid-way (7/6) after rescheduling bugs affected the initial period.

Learnings

What worked

  • The reschedule mechanism worked dramatically — a +3.68pp (+42% relative) jump in reschedule rate, highly stat sig (p<0.0001), which alone nets out to an estimated +2% lift in overall attendance.
  • The redesigned branch runs on more efficient comms infrastructure, which should make it easier to build on going forward.

What didn't

  • Immediate show rate on the first attempt was flat to slightly down (48.12% → 47.95%, not stat sig) — the win here is entirely in capturing people who would've otherwise been lost, not in getting more people to show up the first time.
  • Had to restart mid-test after rescheduling bugs distorted the early read — worth sanity-checking infrastructure before trusting early Super Test numbers.

Follow-up actions

  1. Push the test branch to 100% and finish the appointment ecosystem cleanup.
  2. Move on to match-rate improvement concepts next.

Additional notes

  • Test was restarted on 7/6 after the test branch drove a significant amount of reschedule volume and hit rescheduling bugs during the initial period — results reflect the post-restart read.
