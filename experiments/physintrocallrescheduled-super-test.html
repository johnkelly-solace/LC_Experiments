Impact summary

Performance came back flat — attended rate barely moved (41.11% control vs. 41.54% improved flow, +1% relative, not a meaningful lift) — but the patient experience is better with the cleaned-up flow. Moving forward with the improved flow anyway and shifting focus to match-rate testing next.

Learnings

What worked

  • The improved flow delivers a better patient experience (cleaner touchpoints) without hurting attendance — a safe upgrade even without a measurable show-rate lift.
  • Combining multiple variants into one test kept the pace of iteration up on this flow, matching the same ‘bigger swing’ instinct used on the Booked and Cadence super tests.

What didn't

  • Didn't hit the 10% show-rate lift target — performance came back essentially flat (41.11% vs. 41.54%), unlike the Booked flow's Super Test where reschedule capture drove a real (if modest) attendance gain.
  • A mid-test bug required pushing optimizations into the test branch (7/2) to limit the impact — worth factoring into how much confidence to put in the early read.

Follow-up actions

  1. Roll forward with the improved flow (better experience, no downside) and move on to match-rate testing.
  2. Continue optimizing for the second time through the reschedule ecosystem.
