Progress Bar in Commercial Appt Reminders

Owner team: Appointment Lifecycle Ran: 2026-04-15 → 2026-06-12 Primary metric: Show rate
NEUTRAL

Hypothesis

By adding a progress bar, we'll show patients how close they are to matching with an advocate and increase intent to attend these calls.
Target impact: 5% lift in show rate.

Experiment Details

Adds a visual progress bar to commercial appointment reminders, showing patients how close they are to matching with an advocate.

Impact summary

Learnings

  • Directionally positive — the concept (showing proximity to matching) seems sound.

What didn't

  • Didn't reach the 5% target impact with confidence; execution/visual design likely limited the effect.
